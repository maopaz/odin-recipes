FRIES

French fries are batonnet- or julienne-cut potatoes that are deep-fried until they achieve a golden, crisp exterior and a warm, fluffy interior. Believed to have originated in either Belgium or France, they are one of the most popular side dishes and snacks globally.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Cut and Dry: Slice your potatoes into even strips and dry them thoroughly with a paper towel. (Skip the soaking step to save time).
  2. The Single Fry: Heat your oil to 360°F (180°C). Fry the potatoes in small batches for about 6 to 8 minutes until they are golden-brown and cooked through.
  3. Season: Scoop them out, drain them quickly, and toss them immediately with salt while they are still hot.
