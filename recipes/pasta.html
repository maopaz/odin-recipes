PASTA

Pasta is a traditional Italian staple food made from an unleavened dough of wheat flour (usually durum wheat semolina) mixed with water or eggs.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Garlic
  • Olive Oil

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta: Cook your pasta in a pot of salted boiling water according to the package instructions, then drain it.
  2. Make the quick sauce: While the pasta cooks, heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat, fry the minced garlic for 1 minute until fragrant, then pour in the tomato sauce and let it simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Combine and serve: Toss your drained pasta directly into the pan with the sauce, mix well for 1 minute so it coats evenly, and serve.
