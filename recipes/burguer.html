BURGUER

A burger (short for hamburger) is a popular food consisting of a round patty of cooked ground meat placed inside a sliced bun or bread roll.

Ingredients

  • Beef: 80/20 ground chuck (shaped into round patties)
  • Seasoning: Salt and black pepper.
  • Cheese: Cheddar or American slices.
  • Buns: Sliced brioche or sesame seed buns.
  • Toppings: Lettuce, tomato, onion, and pickles.
  • Sauces: Ketchup, mustard, or mayonnaise.

Steps

  1. Prep: Shape the ground beef into patties slightly wider than your buns. Press a small indent into the center of each to keep them flat while cooking.
  2. Sear: Heat your skillet or grill to high. Season both sides of the patties generously with salt and pepper. Cook for 3-4 minutes until a deep brown crust forms.
  3. Flip and Melt: Flip the patties. Immediately place a cheese slice on top. Cook for another 2-3 minutes until the cheese melts and the meat is cooked through.
  4. Assemble: Spread your sauces on the bottom bun. Layer the lettuce, tomato, pickles, and onion. Place the cheesy patty on top, close with the top bun, and serve.
